What we stand for

Open by default

Papers, code, data and models are released openly, so anyone can check, reuse and build on them.

For all of humanity

We pick problems by who they help. Safety and real-world benefit come before hype.

Independent and democratic

No university, company or investor decides what we study. Members propose and choose the work together.

Credit for everyone

Every contributor is named. You do not need a title, a lab or a PhD to do serious research here.

Research

We focus on AI itself, and apply it wherever it can do the most good.

01

Open models and tools

Models, datasets and training recipes that anyone can reproduce on modest hardware.

02

AI for people

Applying AI to health, education, climate and public services, with the communities who use it.

03

Safe and accountable AI

Evaluation, transparency and safety research that keeps powerful systems answerable to the public.

04

Embodied and efficient AI

Robots and agents that work in the open world, and training methods small labs can afford.

About

Forall Labs was started in Toronto by independent researchers who saw how hard it is to do good AI research without a big institution behind you: no affiliation, no compute, no funding. We are building the place we wished existed, one that is open to everyone and answers only to its members and the public.

∀ is the mathematical symbol for “for all”.

About

An institution built the way we wished one existed.

Forall Labs is an independent, non-profit, open research community in AI, started in Toronto in 2026.

Why it exists

Doing good AI research without a big institution behind you is unreasonably hard: no affiliation, no compute, no funding, and often no way to get the work read. At the same time, more and more of the field's most consequential research happens inside organisations that owe the public no explanation of how it was done.

Forall Labs is a response to both problems at once. It is open to anyone who wants to do the work, and everything it produces is published so anyone can check it.

What the name means

∀ is the mathematical symbol for “for all”: the universal quantifier, the claim that something holds for every member of a set, without exception. That is the ambition, in one character — research that is open to all, and aimed at all.

How it is governed

Members propose the work and choose it together, in the open. No university, company or investor has a say in what gets studied. As the community grows, the governance will be written down properly and published here rather than left to custom.

We would rather say plainly what is still undecided than pretend to a structure we have not built yet. Today the lab is small enough that decisions are made by whoever is doing the work, in public.

How it is funded

It is not, yet. The lab runs on volunteered time, free tiers and free research-credit programmes. Nobody is paid, including the founder. If that changes it will be said openly here, and funding will go to projects and compute before anything else.

How we license

Code is released under permissive open-source licences. Papers, documentation and the text of this site are CC BY 4.0. Models and datasets are released openly with their provenance documented. Contributors keep authorship of their work.

Contact

The lab's email goes live with the forall-labs.ai domain. Until then, the reliable way to reach us is through the GitHub organisation — open an issue or a discussion and it will be seen.

Still being built, in public.

No papers, no running projects, and a community of approximately one. If you want this to exist, the useful thing you can do is join it.

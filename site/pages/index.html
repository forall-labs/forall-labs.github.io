01

Open models and tools

Models, datasets and training recipes anyone can reproduce on modest hardware.

02

What we value

The principles we hold ourselves to.

  1. 01

    Publish everything.

    Papers, code, data and models are released openly, so anyone can check them, reuse them and build on them. A closed result needs a reason.

  2. 02

    Choose work by who it helps.

    Real-world benefit and safety come before novelty, and before results that only look impressive.

  3. 03

    Decide together, in the open.

    No university, company or investor sets the agenda. Members propose the work and choose it together.

  4. 04

    Credit everyone who contributes.

    Every contributor is named. A title, an affiliation or a PhD is not required to do serious research here.

  5. 05

    Report what did not work.

    Negative results are results. Writing them up saves the next person the same months.

How it works

A project here starts with a proposal, not a permission.

Anyone proposes

Write up an idea: what it is, who it helps, what it needs. No affiliation required, and no committee to get past first.

A team forms

Proposals are shared openly so collaborators can find them. Projects that attract people get going.

Everything is published

Code, data and the paper go out openly, with every contributor named. Negative results get written up too.

What we stand for

Four commitments we are willing to be held to.

Open by default

Papers, code, data and models are released openly, so anyone can check them, reuse them and build on them. Closed results are the exception and need a reason.

For all of humanity

We pick problems by who they help. Real-world benefit and safety come before novelty, and before the kind of result that merely looks impressive.

Independent and democratic

No university, company or investor decides what we study. Members propose the work and choose it together, in the open.

Credit for everyone

Every contributor is named. You do not need a title, an affiliation or a PhD to do serious research here — only the willingness to do the work.

Research

We focus on AI itself, and apply it where it does the most good.

Four themes, chosen because they are places a small, open lab can still do work that matters.

01

Open models and tools

Models, datasets and training recipes anyone can reproduce on modest hardware.

02

AI for people

Health, education, climate and public services, built with the communities who use them.

03

Safe and accountable AI

Evaluation, transparency and safety work that keeps powerful systems answerable to the public.

04

Embodied and efficient AI

Robots and agents that work in the open world, and training methods small labs can afford.

How it works

A project here starts with a proposal, not a permission.

1. Anyone proposes

Write up an idea: what it is, who it helps, what it needs. No affiliation required, and no committee to get past first.

2. A team forms

Proposals are shared openly so collaborators can find them. Projects that attract people get going; the rest stay on the shelf.

3. Everything is published

Code, data and the paper go out openly, with every contributor named. Negative results get written up too.

Propose a project.

Members choose the work together. Describe the idea, who it helps and what it needs, and we will share it with the community to find collaborators. You do not need to be a member first.

What happens next

  1. We read it closely.If something is unclear, we write back with questions rather than a rejection.
  2. It goes to the community.With your permission, the proposal is shared so members can sign up to help.
  3. A team forms.Once at least two people commit to owning parts of it, the project starts in the open.
